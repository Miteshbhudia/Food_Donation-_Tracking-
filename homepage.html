Operations

How the Platform Works

A clear, 3-tier operational model built for absolute distribution clarity.

1. Need Declaration

Area Administrators register localized food supply deficits across sub-counties, listing required quantities and dates.

2. Consignment Pledging

Donors review community shortages, pledge matching items, and automatically receive a verified consignment QR code.

3. Field Verification

Field depots scan the consignment QR code upon intake to transition the cargo status from Pending to Delivered.
